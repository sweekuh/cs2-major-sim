Model assumptions and sensitivity

Seeds follow Valve’s November 2 invitation order. Team strength starts from HLTV’s November 19 Valve points. The model uses a 600-point spread for map win odds, then uses independent maps for best-of-three deciders. Opening matches use market prices directly. Each opening pair retains its average Valve rating. Its full market-implied strength difference carries into later matches. Individual vetoes and substitutions are reflected only through the input ratings and prices.
