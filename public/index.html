Model assumptions and sensitivity

Seeds follow Valve’s November 2 invitation order. Team strength starts from HLTV’s November 19 Valve points. Map win odds follow a logistic curve in Valve points. Past Majors identify its spread only loosely, so tournaments are drawn across spreads from 400 to 3,000 points per tenfold change in odds, weighted by how well each fit earlier later-round results. Best-of-three deciders use independent maps. Opening matches use market prices directly. Each opening pair retains its average Valve rating. Its full market-implied strength difference carries into later matches. Individual vetoes and substitutions are reflected only through the input ratings and prices.
