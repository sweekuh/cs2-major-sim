<objective>
Build `engine/optimizer.py` — the pure decision core that turns a finished `Result`
(marginals + the retained per-sim `sample`) plus the team set into both recommended
ballots, their P(≥5) and E[correct], and the correlated-0-3-in-R1 warning. This is the
headless, streamlit-free, RNG-free layer that Plan 02 wires into the UI. It owns the two
named-critical correctness checks (`test_greedy_is_e_correct_optimal`,
`test_pge5_known_answer`) and the contract that the optimizer **consumes the stored sample
and never re-runs the MC** (ROADMAP Success Criterion 4).

Purpose: after this plan the recommendation math is provable in `pytest` independent of any
UI — exactly the Phase-1 discipline (correctness core first, then the shell).

Output: `engine/optimizer.py` and `tests/test_optimizer.py`.
