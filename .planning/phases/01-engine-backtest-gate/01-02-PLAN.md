is the hard part and the part the backtest gate exists to validate. It groups active teams by record,
ranks within group by difficulty-then-seed, pairs via a rematch-avoiding fold backed by Valve's
verbatim 15-row priority table, falls back gracefully on unmatchable groups, places Bo3 correctly, and
honors a `locked` dict deterministically (the seam Phase 4 rides on).

wave-2 swiss tests (fold key, forced rematch, no-matching fallback, locked) flip from xfail to green.

even when this is silently wrong (wrong fold key, greedy-not-table rematch resolution), so this plan's
unit tests plus the wave-4 backtest are the only proofs of correctness.
