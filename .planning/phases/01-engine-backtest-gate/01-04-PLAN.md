The spine of the phase: prove the engine is correct by reproducing a real past Major stage's pairings
exactly. This is the HARD BLOCKING GATE (swiss-validated, GATE-01..05). The structural invariants pass
even when the engine is silently corrupt, so this backtest — against verbatim Valve rules and a verbatim
Budapest 2025 Stage 1 fixture — is the only check that proves the greedy rematch resolution matches
Valve's priority table, the fold key is difficulty (not seed), and Buchholz is wins−losses.

inconsistent across two fetches (the 1-1 bucket entering R3 disagreed), so the fixture MUST be
transcribed verbatim and reconciled match-by-match before encoding — this plan is `autonomous: false`.
It also budgets the planned GATE-04 fork: if any pairing diverges, swap the greedy fold for the literal
priority-table lookup at the diverging group size.

Purpose: Turn "the engine looks right" into "the engine provably reproduces reality." No sim output is
trusted and Phase 2 does not begin until this gate is green (GATE-05).
Output: tests/fixtures/budapest_2025_stage1.json, tests/test_backtest_budapest_2025.py, and any
GATE-04 fork edits to engine/swiss.py.
