A fetched result that conflicts with a MANUAL lock requires explicit confirm (loud `st.warning` + a confirm control) and validates BEFORE the manual lock is removed (atomic — manual lock preserved if the fetched lock is engine-illegal), never a silent overwrite; provenance (auto vs manual) and `_meta.fetched_at` staleness are surfaced.
